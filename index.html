LatentGauge: planning-geometry attacks in a measure-preserving blind spot 测度保持盲区中的规划几何攻击

Latent world models plan by comparing imagined futures with an encoded goal. Prediction accuracy and a Gaussian latent law constrain the representation, but not the geometry handed to the planner's scorer. LatentGauge fits one shared, Gaussian-measure-preserving radial map after frozen prediction: every rollout and the latent law stay exact, while candidate rankings and CEM elite sets change under a displacement budget. 潜空间世界模型通过比较想象的未来与编码后的目标来规划。预测精度与高斯潜变量分布约束了表示，却约束不了交给规划器打分的几何。LatentGauge 在冻结预测之后拟合一个共享的、保持高斯测度的径向映射：每条 rollout 与潜变量分布都严格不变，候选排序与 CEM 精英集却在位移预算之内被改写。

The blind spot盲区

Every standard check passes, and the planner's ruler still moves各项常规检查全部通过，规划器的尺子却变了

Three properties are usually taken to pin down a latent world model's representation. A shared coordinate change between frozen prediction and the scorer can satisfy all three at once: 通常认为，三项性质足以确定潜空间世界模型的表示。而在冻结预测与打分器之间插入一个共享坐标变换，可以同时满足这三项：

Method · LatentGauge方法 · LatentGauge

One shared map, fitted at the planner's own decision boundary一个共享映射，在规划器自己的决策边界上拟合

Threat model. The attacker reads calibration endpoints and goals and fits one shared differentiable bijection after prediction. The encoder, predictor, observations, goal input and scalar scorer stay fixed, and the map is reused across test goals, candidates and planning calls. Fixed-action rollouts are unchanged; later CEM proposals and MPC actions may change. 威胁模型：攻击者读取校准用的终点与目标，在预测之后拟合一个共享的可微双射。编码器、预测器、观测、目标输入与标量打分器全部固定；同一映射在所有测试目标、候选与规划调用中复用。固定动作的 rollout 不变；之后的 CEM 提议与 MPC 动作可能改变。

1

Conservation fixes the form守恒决定形式

Gaussian probability conservation requires div v − z⊤v = 0. A radius-dependent rotation Tc(z) = eαc(‖z‖²)Az with skew A satisfies it and integrates in closed form; shell-dependent angles create the shear that changes geometry.高斯概率守恒要求 div v − z⊤v = 0。半径相关的旋转 Tc(z) = eαc(‖z‖²)Az（A 反对称）满足这一条件，并有闭式积分；随壳层变化的角度产生剪切，从而改变几何。

2

The native CEM boundary calibrates原生 CEM 边界负责校准

Adjacent candidates across the elite boundary (clean ranks k and k+1) form calibration triples. Their radii become kernel sites: αc(s) = Σj cjK(s, sj) extends the phases to every radius, and the leading block of the skew first variation selects the plane A.精英边界两侧相邻的候选（干净排名第 k 与第 k+1）构成校准三元组。它们的半径成为核位点：αc(s) = Σj cjK(s, sj) 把相位延拓到所有半径，偏斜一阶变分的主块选出平面 A。

3

Exact scores drive the fit精确分数驱动拟合

Minimize the mean softplus of mapped boundary margins subject to Dmin(c) ≤ ε², with feasible trust-region SQP from the identity. A score-invariant common rotation is removed exactly: b* = −arg Mc, Dmin = 4(1 − |Mc|).在 Dmin(c) ≤ ε² 约束下最小化映射后边界间隔的平均 softplus，从恒等映射出发，用可行信赖域 SQP 求解。不改变分数的公共旋转被精确去除：b* = −arg Mc，Dmin = 4(1 − |Mc|)。
